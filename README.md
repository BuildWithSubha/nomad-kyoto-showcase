# Nomad Foils & Kisen Ceramics Kyoto — Landing Page Showcase

A dual production-grade landing page suite showcasing two distinct design aesthetics:

1. **[Nomad Foils](./nomad.html)**: High-performance electric carbon-fiber hydrofoil boards for open-ocean riding.
2. **[Kisen Ceramics Kyoto](./kyoto.html)**: Handcrafted wood-fired matcha bowls and authentic Japanese teaware.
3. **[Portal Gateway](./index.html)**: Interactive dual-brand showcase and design comparison.

---

## 🌊 Nomad Foils Highlights
- **Live Flight Telemetry HUD (`FlightAssist™`)**: Dynamic wireless throttle simulation.
- **Mission Planning Simulator**: Live interactive calculation of range, swell conditions, and battery efficiency.
- **Interactive Board Anatomy**: Hotspots revealing Toray T1000 carbon composites and whisper-drive motor.
- **Board Configurator & Test Flight Booking**: Custom spec matrix, finish swatches, and reservation flow.

---

## 🍵 Kisen Ceramics Kyoto Highlights
- **Interactive Vessel Curator**: Multi-chawan collection with glaze inspection points (Biidoro ash glaze, Hiiro flame scorch, Kodai foot).
- **7-Day Anagama Firing Timeline**: Scrubber tracking 168 hours of pine wood firing up to 1,310°C.
- **Web Audio Tearoom Soundscape**: Procedural Japanese singing bowl / water chime synthesizer.
- **Kiribako Presentation**: Authentic Paulownia wood box and packaging details.
